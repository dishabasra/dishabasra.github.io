Hoot

A student planner that reads your syllabus for you. It pulls class times, deadlines, absence rules and grading weights out of a syllabus, syncs with Moodle, Canvas and Google Calendar, and tells you what to start next. It also has Notion-style notes with flashcards, a lecture recorder, a grade calculator and a GPA tracker.
